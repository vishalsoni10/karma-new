Administration shaped around your program

Karma is focused on the administration behind a well-run event, not just the visible setup. We help organizers define responsibilities, prepare guest information, coordinate check-in and keep operational updates organized. Each service is scoped around the event, and technology is used only where it adds value and can be supported reliably.

A connected event workflow

Registration form and attendee-list preparation

QR check-in workflow planning and attendance reports

ID-card layout, data preparation and printing coordination

Accommodation or room allocation lists with restricted access

Volunteer roster, duties, contacts and shift handover

Approved WhatsApp updates and information-desk workflow

Operational dashboards or post-event reports where supported

Event technology and safety coordination

CCTV rental or surveillance coordination where requested and available

Network, power and device requirements planning with the relevant technical team

Manual backup check-in procedures if connectivity or devices fail

Access controls for guest data and role-based information sharing

Written task list, incident log and end-of-event handover

Why an operations-first approach

Clear responsibilities reduce confusion between committees, volunteers and suppliers.

A single approved schedule helps everyone work from the same information.

Digital guest workflows can reduce repeated manual entry when implemented correctly.

Written scope and transparent reporting make it easier to review what was delivered.

Frequently asked questions

Does Karma only handle decoration and stage setup?

No. Karma’s intended focus is event administration, guest operations, coordination and technology workflows. Specialist setup services are not represented as directly provided unless separately confirmed.

Can you build a custom event registration system?

The requirements can be assessed for a suitable registration and reporting workflow. Specific integrations, payment processing, QR features and hosting depend on the agreed implementation.

Can small and large Jain organizations use the service?

The process can be scoped for a single event, multi-day program or larger gathering after confirming the requirements, staffing and available tools.
