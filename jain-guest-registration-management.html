Designed around your group’s requirements

Large religious gatherings can involve guests arriving at different times, volunteers, accommodation assignments and changing schedules. Karma Event Management can help organizers plan a guest-flow process that fits the event, from pre-event registration through arrival support and departure coordination.

A practical guest journey

Collect only the information needed to administer the event and explain its use to attendees.

Prepare and verify guest lists before printing badges or assigning rooms.

Set up check-in desks and a process for correcting duplicate or missing entries.

Provide volunteers with clear escalation instructions for special cases.

Reconcile check-in lists and hand over records to the authorized organizer after the event.

Privacy and reliability

Access to attendee data should be limited to authorized organizers and staff.

QR codes should expose only the information required for check-in, not unnecessary personal details.

Backup lists and a manual check-in procedure help keep entry moving if devices or connectivity fail.

Features depend on the agreed software, equipment, connectivity and event scope.

Frequently asked questions

Can you help with QR registration and ID cards?

We can plan QR-based registration workflows and coordinate ID card design or printing according to the event’s requirements and available setup.

Can room numbers be included in guest records?

Room allocation lists can be part of the agreed workflow. Access to those lists should be restricted to authorized staff.

Is this useful for multi-day Jain events?

Yes, registration and guest-coordination workflows can be tailored to multi-day programs, subject to the scope agreed with the organizer.
