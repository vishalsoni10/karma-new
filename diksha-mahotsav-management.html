Administration shaped around your program

A Diksha Mahotsav is a deeply meaningful occasion for the family and community. Reliable administration helps guests understand the program, volunteers know their responsibilities and organizers manage changes calmly. Karma Event Management & Hospitality focuses on the operational side of the event: planning, registration, communication, coordination and technology support.

Operations support for Diksha Mahotsav

Program schedule and run-sheet preparation

Guest registration lists and information desk

QR check-in and ID-card printing coordination where required

Volunteer roles, briefings and contact lists

Seating and guest-flow planning with the venue team

Accommodation and arrival/departure list coordination

CCTV and event-technology coordination where requested

Before the event

Confirm the approved religious program, venue, dates and expected attendance.

Create a responsibility matrix for the family, committee, volunteers and suppliers.

Prepare guest information and registration workflow.

Agree the escalation path for schedule changes and special requests.

Test required equipment and keep a manual fallback for registration.

During the event

Coordinate check-in and direct guests to the designated areas.

Keep the approved program schedule and contact list accessible to authorized leads.

Track open tasks and supplier dependencies without interfering with religious proceedings.

Record operational issues and hand over the final attendance or task report to the organizer.

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide religious guidance or decide the ceremony schedule?

No. The family and authorized religious representatives determine religious practices and timing. Karma can help administer the approved schedule and logistics.

Can you manage guest lists and ID cards?

We can coordinate guest-list preparation, QR registration and ID-card printing according to the agreed requirements and available equipment.

Do you provide decoration, catering or sound directly?

Karma’s focus is event administration and operations. External specialist services can be coordinated when requested, but their availability, scope and charges must be confirmed with the relevant providers.

Discuss your event requirements

Share the dates, location, expected attendance and operational support needed. We will review the request and confirm availability and scope.
