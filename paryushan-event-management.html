Administration shaped around your program

Paryushan schedules may include daily programs, pravachans, Pratikraman, community information and special arrangements directed by the Sangh. Karma Event Management helps organize the operational details defined by the committee, with respect for the program and its traditions.

Daily program administration

Approved daily timetable and run-sheet distribution

Guest information and attendance workflow

Volunteer roster, contact tree and shift handover

Entry, seating and movement planning with venue representatives

Announcements and WhatsApp information coordination where authorized

Guest support desk and issue escalation

Technology or surveillance coordination where requested

A consistent multi-day process

Confirm the religious schedule with the authorized committee.

Publish only approved event information and updates.

Brief volunteers before each program day and record changes.

Keep attendance and operational records accessible only to authorized staff.

Review incidents, unresolved requests and next-day tasks with the designated lead.

Respectful communication

Use the language and terminology preferred by the organizing Sangh.

Avoid publishing private attendee details or unapproved photographs.

Ensure schedule changes are verified by the authorized organizer before sharing.

Keep religious decisions with the designated religious representatives and committee.
