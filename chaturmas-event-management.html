Planning around your event’s needs

Chaturmas brings communities together for an extended period of religious observance and programs. Practical coordination can help attendees, volunteers and organizers understand the schedule and move through the venue smoothly. Karma Event Management & Hospitality works with the local committee to define requirements and coordinate the agreed event services.

Chaturmas program support

Program-day schedules and coordination with committee representatives

Venue readiness, seating plans and attendee movement

Guest registration and information-desk planning

Volunteer and supplier coordination

Audio, visual display and LED production coordination where needed

Guest hospitality and accommodation coordination where required

Pure vegetarian catering coordination according to organizer instructions

Before the program

Confirm dates, venue, expected attendance and the program timetable.

Identify responsibilities for organizers, volunteers and service providers.

Plan entry, seating, accessibility and information flow for attendees.

Review equipment, power, production and contingency requirements with relevant vendors.

During the program

Coordinate agreed setup and supplier arrival times.

Keep event operations aligned with the approved schedule.

Route questions and changes to the designated committee contact.

Review daily requirements when the program spans multiple days.

Frequently asked questions

Do you plan multi-day Chaturmas programs?

We can discuss multi-day coordination and the practical requirements for each program day. Dates, staffing and availability must be confirmed.

Can you arrange audio-visual support?

Audio-visual and LED production coordination can be included when required and agreed in the scope.

How do we start planning?

Share the program dates, location, approximate attendance and a committee contact so the requirements can be reviewed.

Discuss your event requirements

Share the event type, date, venue, expected attendance and the services you need. We will review the requirements and confirm availability and scope.
