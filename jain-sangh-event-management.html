Planning around your event’s needs

Sangh events often involve many attendees, volunteers, suppliers and moving parts. A clear plan for schedules, guest information, venue flow and responsibilities can reduce last-minute confusion. Karma Event Management & Hospitality works with committee representatives to understand the event and coordinate the services agreed for that gathering.
