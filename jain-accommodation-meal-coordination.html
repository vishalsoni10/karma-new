Frequently asked questions

Can you arrange pure Jain meals?

We can coordinate catering requirements with providers based on the organizer’s written instructions. Menu, preparation practices and availability must be verified directly with the selected provider.

Can you handle group accommodation for a Palitana yatra?

We can discuss guest counts, room lists and accommodation-provider coordination. Rooms and rates are subject to provider confirmation.

When should we contact you?

Contact us as early as possible with dates, group size and the accommodation or meal requirements so options can be checked.
